02 — Workflow

CFD-to-Test Development Workflow

The experience followed a practical motorsport development logic: prepare geometry, run or review CFD, interpret flow structures, identify performance changes, and prepare the work for validation through model-scale wind-tunnel testing.

1. Geometry preparation

Used Siemens NX surfacing and model review to support aerodynamic surfaces suitable for CFD and wind-tunnel model preparation.

2. CFD assessment

Reviewed STAR-CCM+ flow-field outputs including local pressure, velocity and streamline behaviour around aero devices.

3. Component interpretation

Evaluated how local aero-device behaviour influenced flow quality, wake interaction and overall efficiency.

4. Validation planning

Supported pressure-tapping and wind-tunnel correlation planning to connect CFD predictions with physical testing.
