02 — Workflow

Simulation-to-Insight Workflow

The main value of the internship was learning how vehicle dynamics simulation fits into engineering decision-making inside an OEM. The workflow connected simulation setup, repeatable execution, post-processing and engineering interpretation.

1. Model setup and execution

Worked with MSC ADAMS models and simulation procedures for vehicle dynamics and brake-related studies.

2. Response extraction

Reviewed simulation outputs relevant to K&C behaviour, handling response and parking-brake performance.

3. Variant comparison

Used MATLAB and Excel to compare outputs across vehicle configurations and identify behaviour changes.

4. Engineering conclusion

Converted simulation data into practical observations that could support system-level engineering review.
