02 — Workflow

Design-Build-Test Workflow

The experience followed a hands-on Formula Student workflow: understand the vehicle requirement, package the system, support drawings and manufacture, assemble and inspect components, then prepare the vehicle or subsystem for testing.

1. Requirement and packaging review

Considered steering, suspension, ergonomics, safety and vehicle packaging constraints during integration work.

2. Drawing and manufacture support

Supported 2D drawings and practical manufacture-readiness for upright components.

3. Assembly and inspection

Supported assembly checks and ultrasonic NDT inspection to evaluate component condition and fatigue-related concerns.

4. Testing and iteration

Connected manufactured hardware, sensor packaging and practical vehicle testing needs through a design-build-test mindset.
